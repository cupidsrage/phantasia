Deeds

Deeds are lifetime achievements of your account. Every deed you earn gives all your characters a small permanent bonus, and many deeds have several tiers, each adding the bonus again.

DeedEarned byTiersBonus per tier
Monster Slayermonsters slain100 / 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000+1% experience
Champion's Banechampions slain5 / 50 / 250+5% gear finds
Stormridermonsters slain during world events100 / 1,000+1% experience and +1% gold
Dutifuldaily quests completed7 / 30 / 100 / 365+1% gold
Relic Hunterdifferent artifacts held1 / 5 / 11+5% gear finds
Seasonedhighest level any of your characters reaches100 / 500 / 1,000 / 3,000+1% experience
Served as Stewardbecoming Stewardonce+1% gold
Wore the Crownbecoming King or Queenonce+1% gold
Ascended to the Valarbecoming Valaronce+2% experience
Shadowbreakermonsters slain answering a Call to Arms50 / 500 / 5,000+1% experience and +1% gold
Dark Lord's Doomdefeating the Dark Lordonce+2% experience and +2% gold

With every deed complete, your characters gain +16% experience from fights, +12% gold from treasure and 30% more gear finds.

Good to know

  • Progress counts across all the characters of your account, and the bonuses apply to every one of them, including characters you make later.
  • Deed bonuses add to the other bonuses in the realm: Blood Moons, Gold Rushes, the rewards of ruling and your gear.
  • Type /deeds in chat to see your progress and your total bonus. When you earn a deed it is announced in your log and chat.
  • Game wizards using their powers don't earn deeds.
