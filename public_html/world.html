The regions

Shadow creeps over every region unless adventurers fight there. Under heavy Shadow a region is Blighted: its monsters are stronger and richer, and when it is total its town falls. Push it back and the region is Liberated: safer roads and cheaper shops. How the living realm works ›

Where to find shops

Trading posts stand along the road that runs through the middle of each region, at the same distances from the throne on every road, and so in the same circles. The gold dots on the map mark them. Each region's posts belong to its town, so how the town is doing sets their prices (and a fallen town's posts are in ruins). When you enter a region the game tells you where its nearest post is; /realm in chat tells you again.

The 5th posts are in the Plateau of Gorgoroth; the diagonal ones there are the only posts that sell blessings. Posts go on farther out, at 100 × n × n along the diagonals. More about trading posts ›
