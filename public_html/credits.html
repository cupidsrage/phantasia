Phantasia Reimagined

Revived in 2026 by the realm's current keepers, with a browser client, gear and one-of-a-kind artifacts, daily quests, champions, world events, royal regalia and decrees, and 3D ceremonies. Thanks to every player who adventures here.

The 3D scenes use three.js (MIT licence). Fonts are Cinzel and EB Garamond from Google Fonts.
