Gear and Artifacts

Shields, swords and quicksilver have become real equipment.

Slots | Tiers | Abilities | Getting gear | Bags | Wear and loss | Artifacts | Legends (who holds what)

Slots

You wear one item in each of five slots and carry more in your bag: 6 to start, up to 50 with the best bag. In the browser client, click an item in your bag to wear it and click a worn item (on your adventurer or in the list) to take it off. You can't change gear in the middle of a fight. In any client, Info › Gear does the same and also throws items away, and /gear in chat lists them. Taking armor off lowers your current energy by its bonus, and putting it on raises it again, so swapping gear never heals you. Your equipped items add together into the numbers the game has always used: energy (once the shield), damage (once the sword) and quicksilver (speed).

Each item's bonus is set by the circle (C) it comes from and its tier's quality (q). Energy and damage follow how hard monsters hit at that circle (H, below), so gear keeps pace as you move out: a Fine armor and helm together soak up about seven average hits wherever they were found.

SlotBonus
Weapon+0.4 × H × q damage
Armor+5.5 × H × q energy
Helm+2 × H × q energy
Boots+0.6 × (C − 3) × q quicksilver (at least circle 1)
Trinket+0.8 × H × q energy and +0.08 × H × q damage

Each item rolls within ±15% of these values. H, the average monster hit, by circle (in between, it scales smoothly; beyond 100 it grows with the circle):

For example, Fine armor gives about 385 energy from circle 10, 8,000 from circle 30 and 32,000 from circle 100.

Abilities

Combat abilities work against monsters, not other players. Insight and Arcana raise your brains and magic level everywhere. The same ability on several items adds up. "Strength" is 1 for Fine, 1.5 for Masterwork, 2 for Enchanted and 3 for an artifact.

Helms and trinkets roll a magic ability half the time; every other ability can appear on any slot. Brains and magic level from gear count only while the item is worn: your own stats are never changed, so nothing is lost or doubled when you take it off. Focus applies to spells you cast (including teleport and cloak), not to mana that monsters drain.

Getting gear

SourceWhat you get
Monsters7% chance of an item after each kill
Championsalways drop Masterwork or Enchanted gear (as if 2 circles farther out), with an 8% chance of an artifact instead
Treasurefinds that were once shields, bucklers, swords and quicksilver now give armor, helms, weapons and boots made for the circle you are in; 2% of them are an artifact instead
Daily questshalf of all quests, and every champion quest, also reward Fine or Masterwork gear (30% Masterwork) with an ability
Trading poststhe Armory sells 6 pieces a day for your circle: 2 Common, 3 Fine, 1 Masterwork. It buys an item for about half a treasure find of gold from the circle the item came from: (15 + 18 × (C − 1)) × the tier's worth (Worn ¼, Common ½, Fine 1, Masterwork 2, Enchanted 4), × 0.6 for helms and trinkets, × 1.3 with an ability. It sells for 16 times that. Selling what you find adds a little to your gold; it is not a way to get rich.
Fallen adventurersa corpse yields Worn gear: half its weapon and armor, a quarter of its quicksilver

When you find an item you see it beside what you are wearing and can Equip it, Stow it in your bag, or Leave it (or Swap it for what you're wearing when your bag is full). The guru's blessing and the smith now enchant the armor, weapon or boots you are wearing.

Bags

Everyone starts with a Belt Pouch. Every trading post has a Bags counter where you can buy the next bag up. Upgrades go one step at a time, and your items move into the new bag. A bag is lost if your character dies.

A bigger bag also carries more gold. Gold (and gems, which count half) only slows you down once you carry more than your limit: 1,000 gold plus your level times your class's gold tote (Magic-User 150, Fighter 225, Elf 125, Dwarf 200, Halfling 175, Experimento 175), multiplied by your bag's gold factor below. A level 10 Fighter with a Belt Pouch can carry 3,250 gold freely; with a Bag of the Endless Road, 39,000.

Artifacts

Only one of each artifact exists. Each has two abilities at triple strength, and its bonus is rolled at quality 2.6 for the circle where it is found. The whole realm hears when one is found or lost, and the Legends page shows who holds each one right now. An artifact can only be found once you reach the circle listed.
