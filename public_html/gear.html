Slots

You wear one item in each of five slots and carry more in your bag: 6 to start, up to 50 with the best bag. Open Info › Gear in the game to equip, take off or throw away items, or type /gear in chat for a list. Your equipped items add together into the numbers the game has always used: energy (once the shield), damage (once the sword) and quicksilver (speed).

Each item's bonus grows with the circle (C) it comes from, times its tier's quality (q):

SlotBonus
Weapon+1.6 × C × q damage
Armor+40 × C × q energy
Helm+15 × C × q energy
Boots+0.6 × (C − 3) × q quicksilver (at least circle 1)
Trinket+6 × C × q energy and +0.3 × C × q damage

Each item rolls within ±15% of these values.

The farther out you are, the better the odds: every 16 circles shifts about 10% of finds up a tier, to a maximum of 25% by circle 40.

Abilities

Abilities work against monsters, not other players. The same ability on several items adds up. "Strength" is 1 for Fine, 1.5 for Masterwork, 2 for Enchanted and 3 for an artifact.

Getting gear

SourceWhat you get
Monsters7% chance of an item after each kill
Championsalways drop Masterwork or Enchanted gear (as if 2 circles farther out), with an 8% chance of an artifact instead
Treasurefinds that were once shields, bucklers, swords and quicksilver now give armor, helms, weapons and boots with the same bonus; 2% of them are an artifact instead
Daily questshalf of all quests, and every champion quest, also reward Fine or Masterwork gear (30% Masterwork) with an ability
Trading poststhe Armory sells 6 pieces a day for your circle: 2 Common, 3 Fine, 1 Masterwork. Price: energy × 5 + damage × 500 + quicksilver × 2000 gold, × 1.5 with an ability. It buys items from your bag for 40% of that.
Fallen adventurersa corpse yields Worn gear: half its weapon and armor, a quarter of its quicksilver

When you find an item you see it beside what you are wearing and can Equip it, Stow it in your bag, or Leave it (or Swap it for what you're wearing when your bag is full). The guru's blessing and the smith now enchant the armor, weapon or boots you are wearing.

Bags

Everyone starts with a Belt Pouch. Every trading post has a Bags counter where you can buy the next bag up. Upgrades go one step at a time, and your items move into the new bag. A bag is lost if your character dies.

Artifacts

Only one of each artifact exists. Each has two abilities at triple strength, and its bonus is rolled at quality 2.6 for the circle where it is found. The whole realm hears when one is found or lost, and the Legends page shows who holds each one right now. An artifact can only be found once you reach the circle listed.
