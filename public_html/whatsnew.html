Gear and artifacts

Shields, swords and quicksilver are now real equipment: a weapon, armor, a helm, boots and a trinket, each with its own bonus, and many with a special ability such as Evasion, Lifesteal or Crushing Blows. Better gear comes from farther out, champions always drop something good, and trading posts have an Armory. Eleven one-of-a-kind artifacts exist, only one of each. Find one and the whole realm will know. Your old shield, sword and quicksilver became matching "Worn" gear.

All the numbers are on the Gear and Artifacts page, and the Legends page shows who holds each artifact right now.

Daily quests

Every character gets one quest a day. It is shown in your chat window when you enter the realm, and you can check it any time by typing /quest in chat. Quests change at midnight UTC. Progress is shown in chat as you go.

QuestGoalHow often
Slay monstersSlay 6 to 12 monsters30%
Slay deepSlay 4 to 8 monsters at least 1 to 3 circles beyond where you stood when the quest was given25%
JourneyReach a circle 3 to 6 beyond where you stood when the quest was given (at least circle 3)20%
Treasure huntDiscover treasure 3 to 5 times17%
Champion slayerDefeat a champion8%

Rewards, based on your level when you finish. Half of all quests, and every champion quest, also give a piece of gear.

GoldGems
Most quests200 + 60 × level1 + level ÷ 25
Champion slayer3 × (200 + 60 × level)2 × (1 + level ÷ 25)

For example, a level 10 character earns 800 gold and 1 gem, or 2,400 gold and 2 gems for slaying a champion. The whole realm hears when you complete your quest.

Champions

Any ordinary monster met at random has a 3% chance of being a champion: a named, battle-hardened version of that monster, such as Grishnak the Troll. During a Champion Hunt the chance rises to 15%. Special monsters (the Dark Lord, Modnar, unicorns and so on) are never champions, and champions never flock. The whole realm is told when a champion appears and when one is slain.

Defeating a champion also wins its hoard: 250 to 500 gold per size and 1 + size ÷ 4 gems, plus up to 3 more. A champion's size is the circle you meet it in, like any monster.

World events

Every so often (on average every @@EVENT_MINUTES@@ minutes, and anywhere from half to one and a half times that) a world event begins and lasts @@EVENT_LENGTH@@ minutes. It is announced to everyone in chat when it starts and when it ends. Type /event in chat to see what is happening and how long is left. Events only start while someone is playing.

EventEffect
Blood MoonRandom monster encounters rise from 20% to 32% of turns, and every monster is worth double experience.
Gold RushTreasure is found 3 times as often, and gold found in treasure is doubled.
Champion HuntThe chance of a monster being a champion rises from 3% to 15%.

Each event is equally likely.

New monsters

Twelve new monsters roam the realm, from the Giant Rat near the origin to the Shadow Dragon beyond circle 36. Their full statistics and the circles they live in are on the Monsters page.

Chat commands

Type these into the chat box. Only you see the answer.

/questToday's quest and your progress
/eventThe current world event and how long it lasts
/gearList your gear (change it with Info › Gear)
/artifactsWho holds each artifact
/filterTurn the chat filter on or off (it used to be in the Info menu)
/helpList the commands
