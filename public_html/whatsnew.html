The living realm

The realm now changes with what its adventurers do. See it live on The Realm Today; the rules are on The Living Realm.

  • Shadow and liberation. Eight regions surround the Heartlands. Shadow creeps over each one, and every monster slain there pushes it back. Blighted regions have stronger, richer monsters; liberated ones have safer roads and cheaper shops, and the hero who frees one is named to all.
  • Living towns. Each region's town prospers when you trade and fight nearby: lower prices, and an inn that heals you once it flourishes. New trading posts now stand on the main north, south, east and west roads as well as the diagonals, at the same distances from the throne, so every region has shops. See where every shop is. A town swallowed by Shadow falls, and its trading posts lie in ruins until it is retaken.
  • Sieges on the throne. Every few hours a monster army marches on the Lord's Chamber. Every kill anywhere pushes it back. Win, and the defenders are paid; lose, and monsters hold the throne room for a day.
  • Realm projects. The whole realm builds great works together with gold (the new Realm Project button at trading posts) and kills. Each one finished helps everyone forever.
  • Nemeses. A monster that kills an adventurer can become a named nemesis that grows with every victim, roams the realm and carries a bounty on its head.
  • Monster ecology. Hunt one kind of monster hard and its neighbours grow bold. Rare creatures like the Golden Stag appear only when the conditions are right.
  • Day, night and seasons. Night falls one hour in three, and each week brings a new season with its own effect. The game shows both at the top of the screen.

Call to Arms, and richer Shadow

The regions in the worst state are now the most rewarding:

  • The darker, the richer. Every kill gives more experience and gold the deeper its region lies in Shadow: +1% for every 2% of Shadow past 25%, up to +37%.
  • Call to Arms. Every hour the region deepest in Shadow is called. Kills there earn Valor (bonus gold), and count toward a new deed, Shadowbreaker. The call is shown at the top of the game screen, in /realm and on The Realm Today.

Deeds

Lifetime achievements of your account, each giving all your characters a small permanent bonus: slay 100 monsters for +1% experience, become King or Queen once for +1% gold, and many more, up to +16% experience, +12% gold and 30% more gear in all. Type /deeds to see your progress. Details on the Deeds page.

Rewards for the realm's rulers

Holding a title is now worth fighting for:

  • Regalia that grow with your level and are worn on top of your gear: the Steward's Seal, the King's or Queen's Crown, and the Halo of the Valar.
  • Richer fighting: up to 50% more experience and gold, and more and better gear.
  • The royal treasury pays the Steward and the ruler a stipend; a tenth of all shop spending flows into it, and collecting taxes no longer adds sin.
  • New decrees: the Steward can Feed the Poor, the King or Queen can Call an Event, and the Valar can Bless the Whole Realm.

See the Steward, the King or Queen and the Valar, and type /court to see who rules and how full the coffers are.

New spells

From magic level 10, the spell menu gains a More Spells page: Frost Lance, Mending Light, Drain Life, Fireball and Banish. Costs and effects are on the Spells page.

Colour-coded log and chat

The browser game now colours what happens so you can follow a fight at a glance: monsters that attack you, damage you take, blows you land, healing, gold, gems, gear and experience, travel and the state of the realm, and big announcements. Gear is shown in its tier's colour wherever it is named (in shops, finds and rewards), and a shop's buttons match the items they buy: Worn, Common, Fine, Masterwork, Enchanted and artifacts. In chat, every player's name has its own colour, messages that mention you are highlighted, announcements stand out, and the game's answers to your commands are shown more quietly.

Arrange your buttons

In the browser game, click Arrange buttons to put the action buttons wherever suits your play style. Each character keeps its own layout, so your fighter can keep Melee first and your magic-user Spell. See The Game Screen.

Ceremonies in 3D

When a wizard enters the realm, or someone becomes Steward, King, Queen or Valar, every player in the browser game sees a full-screen 3D ceremony. World events and sieges are announced the same way, and while one lasts its scene plays behind the game.

Gear and artifacts

Shields, swords and quicksilver are now real equipment: a weapon, armor, a helm, boots and a trinket, each with its own bonus, and many with a special ability such as Evasion, Lifesteal or Crushing Blows. Better gear comes from farther out, champions always drop something good, and trading posts have an Armory. Eleven one-of-a-kind artifacts exist, only one of each. Find one and the whole realm will know. Your old shield, sword and quicksilver became matching "Worn" gear.

All the numbers are on the Gear and Artifacts page, and the Legends page shows who holds each artifact right now.

Champions

Any ordinary monster met at random has a 3% chance of being a champion: a named, battle-hardened version of that monster, such as Grishnak the Troll. During a Champion Hunt the chance rises to 15%. Special monsters (the Dark Lord, Modnar, unicorns and so on) are never champions, and champions never flock. The whole realm is told when a champion appears and when one is slain.

World events

Every so often (on average every @@EVENT_MINUTES@@ minutes, and anywhere from half to one and a half times that) a world event begins and lasts @@EVENT_LENGTH@@ minutes. It is announced to everyone in chat when it starts and when it ends. Type /event in chat to see what is happening and how long is left. Events only start while someone is playing.

EventEffect
Blood MoonRandom monster encounters rise from 20% to 32% of turns, and every monster is worth double experience.
Gold RushTreasure is found 3 times as often, and gold found in treasure is doubled.
Champion HuntThe chance of a monster being a champion rises from 3% to 15%.
Siege of the ThroneA monster army marches on the throne for 45 minutes. Every monster slain anywhere cuts it down. See sieges.

Blood Moons, Gold Rushes and Champion Hunts are equally likely. Sieges come on their own schedule, every few hours. A King or Queen can also call an event by royal decree.

New monsters

Twelve new monsters roam the realm, from the Giant Rat near the origin to the Shadow Dragon beyond circle 36. Their full statistics and the circles they live in are on the Monsters page.
